Things people usually ask.

Do I have to hire you for everything?

No. Most people start with one thing. If you need more later, you won’t have to bring someone new up to speed.

Can you work with the site I already have?

Usually, yes. WordPress, Squarespace, Wix, GoDaddy. A lot of web work is fixing what’s there rather than starting over.

How does pricing work?

We talk first. Then you get a written scope, timeline and price before anything starts. If something changes partway through, we talk about it before it turns into a surprise.

Who will I actually be working with?

Me. No account manager, no junior designer. The person on the call is the person doing the work.

Are you taking on new work?

FCS is a part-time studio and most of my work comes through referrals, so I keep a small client list. If someone sent you, mention their name when you reach out.
