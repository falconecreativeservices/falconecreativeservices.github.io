About me

Hi, I’m Paul. I live in Central Massachusetts, and I’ve spent the last 15 years making things.

It started on a stage. I played in Scan the Sky and Sleep Season, and our two albums, Dear Hope and We Must Be Broken, ended up naming something bigger. In 2014 they became Dear Hope, a mental health platform where people shared what they were going through in essays, photos and poems. It reached 140,000 readers and took us to Mental Health America’s national conference in 2016.

These days I work full time in marketing, and I run Falcone Creative Services on the side: a small, referral-only studio for video, web and design.

The thread through all of it is the same. Show up fully, care how it turns out, and make something worth being proud of.

Same lightbulb, three chapters.
