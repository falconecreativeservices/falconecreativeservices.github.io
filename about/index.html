About

Built from something real.

FCS didn’t start with a business plan. It started with two decades of making things — songs, stories, campaigns, films — and the realization that the work had always been the point. Everything since has been building toward a cleaner version of that same idea.

Every client relationship at FCS is treated like a partnership. The work doesn’t get handed off. The opinions are real. The investment is genuine. That’s not a positioning statement. It’s how this has always operated.

Music
2012
The Music Chapter
Where It Started

Two bands. Two albums. One idea forming.

Before there was a studio, there was a stage. Scan The Sky put out an album called Dear Hope in 2012. The name stuck. Then came Sleep Season, and an album called We Must Be Broken in 2014. That album’s cover featured a lightbulb: broken, dim, but still holding its shape. Nobody knew it at the time, but those two album titles were going to become something else entirely.

Music was where the instinct to create first found a home. It taught storytelling under pressure, collaboration across strong opinions, and the particular discipline of making something emotionally honest within a structure. Those lessons never went away.

Advocacy
2015
The Advocacy Chapter
Dear Hope

A mental health platform built from two album titles.

Dear Hope and We Must Be Broken became the foundation of a mental health advocacy platform at wemustbebroken.com. The mission was simple: connect people who were struggling with people who had been there, through personal essays, photography, poetry, and honest storytelling. No clinical distance. No corporate messaging. Just real people sharing real experiences.

What started as a passion project grew into a globally read publication. At its peak, Dear Hope had 1,970 email subscribers and had been featured in Germ Magazine and The Mighty. A remote team of four handled content, editing, and social media. And in 2016, Dear Hope was invited to speak at Mental Health America’s national conference, “Media, Messaging, and Mental Health” in Virginia, where the panel included Ms. Rachel, who has since become one of the most recognized children’s educators in the country. Dear Hope also presented at Westfield State University. The platform ran through 2019.

Career
2019
The Career Chapter
Full-Time Marketing

The creative work found a bigger stage.

From 2019 to 2026, the focus shifted inward. As a Marketing Coordinator and then sole Marketing Manager at a Central Massachusetts B2B manufacturing company, reporting directly to the C-suite, the same creative instincts that built two bands and a global publication got applied to a completely different scale. Strategy, campaigns, web, video, trade shows, email, SEO, collateral. All of it, owned end to end.

One of the biggest early decisions was to bring photo and video production entirely in-house: building a studio, buying equipment, and doing the work directly. Over that tenure, that single decision saved an estimated $500,000+ in vendor costs and put the creative output firmly under one roof where quality could be controlled. A 150+ page website was redesigned and rebuilt in-house. Organic traffic grew nearly 30% year over year. The department that was handed over became something the whole company leaned on.

In 2026, that chapter closed and a new one began: joining a significantly larger organization as a senior member of the marketing team. The work continues full time. Falcone Creative Services runs alongside it, taking on select clients in the hours that belong to the craft.

Studio

The Studio Chapter
Falcone Creative Services

Referral-only. Intentionally small. Part-time by design.

Falcone Creative Services launched in 2023 as a referral-only independent studio. The decision to keep it referral-only was intentional. Not a limitation, but a philosophy. Every client who comes through the door arrived because someone they trust sent them. That changes the dynamic from the first conversation.

The client list is intentionally small. The work is never handed off. Every engagement, whether it’s a single video or a multi-year retainer, is treated like it’s the only one. FCS operates as a part-time independent studio: select clients, intentional hours, and full commitment to every project that comes through. Revenue has grown every year since launch, and the pipeline has never depended on cold outreach.

The work spans video, web, design, content, and strategy. Sometimes all five for the same client. The through-line across all of it is the same thing it’s always been: show up fully, care about the outcome, and make something worth being proud of.

Identity
The Symbol

One lightbulb, three chapters.

The FCS mark is the same lightbulb from a 2014 album cover.
It just kept changing shape.

The lightbulb first appeared on the cover of the Sleep Season album We Must Be Broken in 2014. When Dear Hope launched the following year, a broken, inverted version of it became the symbol for the platform: a light that wasn’t fully working yet, but still had the shape of one.

When Falcone Creative Services launched in 2023, the lightbulb came back. But different. Upright now. A tree growing inside it. A figure sitting beneath the branches. It’s the same image it always was, but the meaning has evolved: an idea taking root. Growth. Wonder. The intent behind the work never changed. The form just kept getting clearer.
